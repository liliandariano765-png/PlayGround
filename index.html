CUPHED

Mug Mayhem — a tiny cup hero vs. Lord Kettle. Run, gun, dash & parry through 3 phases. Built for thumbs & keyboards.

💗 Pink = parryable! Hit L / PARRY near pink shots to bounce & charge your SUPER. Fill the meter, then blast with U / SUPER.

❓ How to play

🎯 Goal: empty Lord Kettle's HP bar before he empties your 5 hearts.

🏃 Move: D-pad / ←→ · Jump: JUMP / Space (hold = higher) · Shoot: hold SHOT / J

💨 Dash (DASH / K) gives brief invincibility — dash through blobs. Cooldown ~0.8s.

💗 Parry: press PARRY / L near pink shots. Destroys them, bounces you up, +34% SUPER, +250 pts.

⚡ Super: at 100%, press SUPER / U for MEGA SIP (26 dmg + clears bullets).

👾 Sugar-cube minions walk at you — shoot them (+150). Boss charges in phases 2–3: jump or dash over him!
